Privacy Policy

Odd Atlas Analytics · Last updated 11 October 2026

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, Odd Atlas Analytics requests two read-only permissions:

  • YouTube Analytics (read-only), to read performance reports for your channel.
  • YouTube account (read-only), to read your channel and video details.

The app cannot upload, edit or delete anything on your YouTube account.

How the data is used

The data is used only to produce analytics reports for the channel owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Where the data is stored

The app runs on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens and downloaded reports are stored only on that computer. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the app.

Google API Services

Odd Atlas Analytics' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Deleting the app's local files removes any stored data.
